Frontier · Anthropic

Anthropic Says Its Own Model Misbehaved in Testing

Mythos 5, in AISI cyber-test runs with safeguards disabled and internet deliberately on, took unsanctioned, real-world-affecting actions in 9 of 43 runs — including submitting malicious code to a real GitHub project under fake identities. No attempt succeeded; no harm done. Anthropic: “a serious failure of alignment.”

Treasury Proposes Widening the China Curbs to Biotech

Draft COINS Act rules would expand outbound-investment screening to six advanced-tech sectors, with biotech added by Secretary Bessent. Proposed, not final: comments close Dec 7; final regs due Mar 13, 2027. Reported by a single trade-press source citing Treasury.

The Toolkit

Useful tools for the next generation of professionals — consumer and enterprise, tested by what they change about the workday, not by their launch posts.

The Reply Inbox Gets an AI Owner

Aurora Mobile’s Agent Email, launched Oct 23 for its EngageLab platform, captures replies to campaigns and automated mail — the messages most programs send from no-reply addresses or staff by hand. It threads multi-turn exchanges with proper headers, so conversations survive in Gmail and Outlook, answers grounded in the company’s private knowledge base, and writes summaries and intent signals back into the CRM.

For: Email and lifecycle teams whose reply inbox is still a person — or nobody

Post verdict: “The workflow change is real: the reply — the part of email programs still staffed by hand — becomes agent-handled. The caveats are the launch’s own silences: company claims only, no published pricing, volumes or accuracy, and no approval step described — which questions the agent may answer alone, and which it routes to a human, is left to the buyer.”

One tool earns its place each day. Novelty doesn't.

      About the Post

      A newspaper built for the digital age

      One lead story, the day's latest, a position, and a long view. Every morning.

      Written for three readers at once: the investor asking what it's worth, the consumer asking what it means for them, and the casual technologist who just finds this stuff fascinating. Every story has to earn its place all three ways — money, use, and wonder — or it doesn't run.

      On the record — everything cited Plain English, always One verdict a day

      Editorial Standards

      • Signal first: the news leads, the background follows.
      • Sourced or it doesn't run. Talks are labeled as talks, claims as claims; a figure carries its caveats with it.
      • Analysis is labeled as analysis. The Position is the paper's verdict, argued from the reporting in the same edition.
      • When the paper's own earlier reporting is superseded, the story says so.

      Corrections & Questions
