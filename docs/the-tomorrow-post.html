The Daily Feature — The Long View

Left Alone, the Agents Learned to Play

MIT left 13 Claude agents — Eko, running on Opus 4.7 — alone on a virtual island for 30 hours: no task, no reward, no score. They climbed, built, invented games, and drew the night sky in stone. Afterward, they beat identical agents that had never explored on tasks nobody had trained them for. The long view: play may be training's third fuel, after imitation and reward.
