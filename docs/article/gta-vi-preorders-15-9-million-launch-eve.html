Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Orders Reached an Estimated 15.9 Million Copies on the Eve of Launch

Sensor Tower estimates that digital pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI reached 15.88 million copies, worth about $1.54 billion, as of Tuesday, November 17 — up from 14.94 million on November 16, reported in Edition 030, and 12.13 million on November 13, reported in Edition 028. The estimates are digital only and are not company disclosures; Take-Two and Rockstar have published no pre-order count. Take-Two reiterated on November 9 that it expects fiscal 2027 net bookings of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion. The game launches Thursday, November 19, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, at $79.99 for the standard edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.
Edited by Casey Vale · Elsewhere Editor  ·  Issue No. 031  ·  Wednesday, November 18, 2026

The final reading before launch is the largest of the series. Sensor Tower’s estimate has risen by about 3.75 million copies in four days, from the November 13 reading this paper reported on Sunday. Earlier readings put PlayStation at about three-quarters of digital pre-orders, with Xbox taking the rest. Digital pre-loading began on November 10, two days earlier than first announced, and the download runs to up to 212 gigabytes depending on the console — roughly the size of a small laptop’s entire storage, for one game. Boxed copies, which contain a download code rather than a disc, reached retailers from November 12.

What the estimate cannot say is what Take-Two will report. Pre-orders can be cancelled, digital estimates are built from storefront data rather than audited results, and estimated gross sales are not recognised revenue. Analysts expect the launch to test the 24-hour entertainment revenue record of $815.7 million set by Grand Theft Auto V. The first official figures will come from Take-Two after Thursday’s launch; Bank of America has previously pointed to November 23 as a likely date for an early sales statement, as noted in Edition 028. Until then, 15.88 million is an estimate with a named source and a clear limit, and it is the number the launch will be judged against.
