Jensen Huang Resigned as Nvidia Chief Executive, Ending 33 Years in the Job

Nvidia announced on Thursday, November 12 that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately. The board had accepted the resignation on November 11, according to the company’s filing. Colette Kress, the finance chief since 2013, is interim chief executive. The announcement came five days before Nvidia reports quarterly results, and four days after the board opened an investigation into whether Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence operation.
Edited by Avery Lane · Frontier Editor  ·  Issue No. 026  ·  Friday, November 13, 2026

Nvidia has lost the only chief executive it has ever had as a public company. The company announced on Thursday that Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately. Its filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission says the board accepted the resignation on Wednesday, November 11. Colette Kress, who has been chief financial officer since 2013, serves as interim chief executive while keeping the finance job. The board named Harvey C. Jones, a director since 1993, as its first chair, and is searching for a permanent successor inside and outside the company, the Financial Times reported.

The resignation ends a week that began in public. On Sunday, November 8, Nvidia said independent directors, helped by outside lawyers, would investigate whether Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence operation. The board said at the time that it had no reason to believe national security interests or Nvidia’s secrets had been compromised. In a memo to employees the same day, Huang acknowledged that he had been unfaithful to his wife and said he took full responsibility. On Wednesday, The New York Times reported a second relationship, with Kimberly Powell, a vice president who reported directly to Huang, citing five people who worked at the company.

What is alleged beyond that remains unproven. United States officials suspect the Singapore-based executive at the centre of the first reports of spying for China, USA Today reported; China has rejected the espionage allegations, Reuters reported, and no public finding has established that any secret changed hands. Huang, who is 63, co-founded Nvidia in 1993 with Curtis Priem and Chris Malachowsky and holds about 3.6% of its shares. The company gave no reason for the resignation in its filing.

The market’s first answer was a fall. Shares fell by more than 13% after the news on Thursday, USA Today reported, and by as much as 14% during the session before recovering part of the loss, according to Reuters. The company, worth roughly $4 trillion, had already shed about $600 billion of market value during the week, USA Today calculated. Nvidia kept its reporting date: third-quarter results arrive on Tuesday, November 17, against the company’s own guidance of $108 billion of revenue, plus or minus 2%. Kress, who has presented those figures to investors for a decade, will now present them as the person in charge.
