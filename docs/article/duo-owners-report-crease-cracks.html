Some Early Duo Owners Report Screens Cracking at the Crease

The sellout survived the weekend — new orders now ship January 25. The crease question the paper set on Friday has a split answer: reviewers call it near-invisible; a cluster of owners say theirs broke at the fold within hours.
Edited by Taylor Brooks · Build &amp; Tools Editor  ·  Issue No. 006  ·  Sunday, October 25, 2026

This is the checkpoint Edition 003 set: does the sellout survive the weekend, and what do first owners say about the crease? The sellout held — pre-orders slipped to a January 25, 2027 delivery estimate within about 30 minutes on October 16, walk-in stock on Friday was “limited,” and Apple called demand “nothing short of extraordinary.”

The crease answer split. Within hours of Friday’s on-sale, owners in Australia, the US, Canada and China posted photos of cracks and bright pixel lines along the inner display near the hinge, the failures consistently located about two-thirds of the way down the crease. The best-documented case: an Australian owner whose screen cracked after fewer than 20 opens and roughly six hours of use, with no drop marks; Apple replaced the device on the spot. Photos of damaged demo units in mainland China also circulated, though MacRumors cautions their backstory is unknown and user damage cannot be excluded.

Apple has not commented publicly and has not confirmed any defect; the number of affected units and any failure rate are unknown, and no rate can be computed from a small, self-selected set of reports against a sold-out launch. Durability tests cut both ways: JerryRigEverything’s unit fared badly — dust entered the hinge despite the IP68 rating, the inner screen scratched at Mohs level 2, and a bend test left a permanent green line before the display stopped responding — while a 10,500-fold test and a 32,000-fold robot test left hinges functional with visibly deepened creases. The standing consensus otherwise holds: the near-invisible crease and matte inner display are the hardware story; the apps — floating, windowed or letterboxed unpredictably — remain the weak point.
