OpenAI’s Bridge Signs Friday, Against a Rival That Has Not Priced

No fresh terms in the window — but the calendar has moved: the Anthropic price some buyers reportedly want to see first now lands in November, after Friday’s deadline.
Edited by Jordan Hale · Capital Editor  ·  Issue No. 008  ·  Tuesday, October 27, 2026

Nothing in the window moves the bridge’s terms: up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, Coatue and UAE investors leading, MGX reported in for up to $10 billion and BlackRock in talks, signatures targeted for Friday, October 30 (CNBC, October 22 — still the freshest hard reporting). OpenAI has declined to comment throughout. Status: talks, not closed.

What has moved is the calendar around one reported reason for hesitation. The Financial Times reported last week that some investors would rather wait for Anthropic’s IPO before committing — and Edition 007 reported the same instinct from the other side of the table. As of Monday, Anthropic has filed; what it has not done is price, and on the fifteen-day clock the price now lands with the roadshow in the week of November 9 — after Friday’s deadline. The wait, if the reporting is right, cannot be satisfied in time. Friday therefore tests something cleaner than it did a week ago: whether $1.25 trillion signs on its own evidence, or whether the round closes smaller, later, or repriced — the three outcomes this paper’s repricing thread has tracked since Edition 003.
