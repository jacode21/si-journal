Grand Theft Auto VI Launched at Midnight, With an Estimated 17.1 Million Copies Already Sold and Take-Two Up About 8%

Grand Theft Auto VI became playable at local midnights around the world on Thursday, November 19, thirteen years after its predecessor. Sensor Tower estimated about 17.1 million copies sold by noon Eastern on Wednesday, worth roughly $1.65 billion — estimates from a tracker, not Take-Two figures. Reuters reported reviewers and players applauding the release, and Take-Two shares rose about 8% on Wednesday. Wells Fargo raised its forecast for the game’s sales in the fiscal year through March to about 65 million copies, from 35 million. The game holds a score of 99 on Metacritic from 147 reviews. It launches as a single-player game, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only, at $79.99.
Edited by Casey Vale · Elsewhere Editor  ·  Issue No. 032  ·  Thursday, November 19, 2026

The rollout was staggered by time zone, so launch day arrived in waves: New Zealand first, India by morning, the United States at midnight Eastern. In India, quick-commerce firm Blinkit began delivering the boxed edition — a box containing a download code — in select cities, a sign of how physical retail now works for a 212-gigabyte game: the disc is a key, and the download is the product. Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick told Reuters pre-orders ran ahead of the company’s expectations. Oppenheimer estimates about $1.85 billion of gross revenue in the first three days, against $1 billion for Grand Theft Auto V in 2013; the 24-hour record set by that game, $815.7 million, is the figure Polymarket bettors priced at a 99% chance of falling.

What is not yet known is the official count. Sensor Tower’s estimates are built from storefront and payment data, and Take-Two has published no unit figure; an early sales statement is expected next week, with Bank of America previously pointing to November 23. Multiplayer, the mode that kept the previous game selling for a decade, is not in the launch version; Bloomberg has reported it is expected in 2027, and Rockstar has announced no date. The first weekend will show whether a single-player launch at $79.99 holds the curve the estimates describe.
