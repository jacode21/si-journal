# The Tomorrow Post — Standards

The paper's standing design and copy standards. Set Oct 27, 2026.

## Lines

- **Tagline (the only one):** "A newspaper for the digital age."
  Used in the masthead, title, og/twitter cards, article header, email,
  and footer. The retired variant "A newspaper built for the digital
  age." appears nowhere.
- **Approved secondary descriptor** (subscribe copy only):
  "A concise, intelligent briefing on what matters next."
  Copywriters use this line rather than improvising a second tagline.

## The horizon mark

The sun over a copper horizon is the paper's only symbol.

- Defined sizes: masthead (44px wide), divider (44px), favicon (16/32px
  from the same path). Never use it at other sizes, never stretch it,
  never recolour it off-palette.
- Clear space on all sides: at least the height of the horizon rule.
- As a section divider it appears **at most once per page** (trial:
  article pages only).

## Theme

- **Default: the paper (light) edition** — Editorial call, Oct 27, 2026,
  superseding the dark default of Oct 23. The dark edition stays one
  tap away in the masthead toggle, and a reader's saved choice always
  wins over the default. Themes change color only, never geometry.

## Palette

- Paper #EDEAE3 · Ink #111417 / #14181D · Accent copper #A65E2E.
- Copper **text**: #8F5127 on paper (5.78:1), #B87333 on the dark
  edition. Accent #A65E2E is for fills and rules, not small text on
  paper-dim backgrounds.

## Type

- Playfair Display (wordmark, headlines), Newsreader (body), IBM Plex
  Mono (labels, folios).
- Body numerals: oldstyle proportional. Tables and data rows: tabular.
- Mobile floors: labels never below 12px, meta never below 13px.
- Tap targets: every link, button, and input at least 44px tall.

## Folio

One folio style on every surface — home, article, The Record, email,
share cards: "Issue No. NNN · Weekday, Month D, YYYY" with the edition
name appended for special editions ("— Evening Edition"). Exception
(Direction A, Oct 27, 2026): the front page's lead meta line shortens
the mark to "No. NNN" set in copper, followed by the date and edition
name separated by "·".

## Mobile proportion refinement (Oct 28, 2026)

Phone widths (≤760px) follow a deliberate type scale, set against a
390–430px viewport: masthead wordmark ~32px (compact since v1001.4 —
do not enlarge), tagline/intro ~17px, navigation labels ~11px with
44px targets, section eyebrows 11–12px, Lead headline 38–42px at
line-height 1.05 on the full column measure (no ch cap), Lead summary
~19px at line-height 1.45, article body 17–19px. One spacing rhythm
between major sections (2.15rem); related elements sit closer. The
subscribe strip's email field carries the placeholder "Your email
address", as the footer and article forms do.

## Refinement build (Oct 28, 2026, late)

Approved from a live mockup (instruction: implement the full
mobile-first refinement brief). Mobile navigation: at 760px and below the
bar carries Today, Topics and The Record; Saved, About and Search move
into a flat More panel (hairline rows, 44px targets, no cards or shadows);
desktop keeps the six-item bar. The masthead subscribe strip carries the
line "The stories shaping tomorrow. Thoughtfully selected, delivered each
morning." with a "Free · One thoughtful briefing each morning" fine line
on mobile; the footer subscribe block keeps the sanctioned descriptor.
Footer is a colophon: mark and wordmark, the mission line, hairline link
rows (About the paper / Standards & corrections / Contact / Subscribe),
and the fine print including the copyright line. Sharing: the article
share panel carries a Copy link button, and the device-share control
falls back to copying the link where no device sheet exists (this
supersedes the earlier no-copy-link rule). "The Bigger Picture" is an
optional synthesis block on a Lead article only — two or three plain
sentences, used only where the reporting supports it; never mechanical.
