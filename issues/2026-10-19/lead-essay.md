# The Grid Said No

_Microsoft walked away from Britain's flagship AI data centre. The chips were ready. The power wasn't. Get used to that sentence._

## The short version

Microsoft walked away from Britain's flagship AI data centre because the grid can't connect it until the 2030s. Meanwhile, Anthropic signed for Canadian capacity with its own wind farms on the way.
The constraint on intelligence is turning into electricity, and the patient capital that can supply it.

## What happened

On Sunday the Telegraph first reported that Microsoft had pulled out as the expected anchor tenant of Nscale's planned AI data centre in Loughton, Essex ([Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/business/2026/10/18/microsoft-axes-ai-deal-setback-uk-data-centre-pioneer/); [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/18/microsoft-drops-huge-ai-datacentre-project-in-essex-over-power-supply-concerns)).

Starmer's government had billed it as the "largest UK sovereign AI datacentre" ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/18/microsoft-drops-huge-ai-datacentre-project-in-essex-over-power-supply-concerns)). Nscale pledged £2bn to it ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9m26kl5y7epo)). Microsoft was its only publicly announced customer ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/18/microsoft-drops-huge-ai-datacentre-project-in-essex-over-power-supply-concerns)).

The problem was the grid. UK Power Networks told Nscale it couldn't supply enough power until the early-to-mid 2030s ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/18/microsoft-drops-huge-ai-datacentre-project-in-essex-over-power-supply-concerns)). Nscale told the BBC it is "exploring alternative power sources" and that the "London-wide grid issue has no impact on our other contracts." Microsoft declined to comment ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9m26kl5y7epo)).

## Why it matters

For years, AI was a story about chips. Lately it's a story about wires. Wires take time, and time is the one input nobody can buy more of.

Loughton isn't alone. The FT counts at least 47 large US data-centre projects delayed, withdrawn or blocked this year, and Bernstein thinks only about 35% of the US pipeline is credible ([Hindustan Times, summarizing the FT](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/americas-10-trillion-ai-data-centre-boom-faces-delays-rising-costs-and-investor-concerns-101792343327657.html)). Data-centre executives say some regulators now refuse grid connections unless a project brings its own power (same).

Credit is tightening, too. CoreWeave, one of the leading neoclouds, carries about $50bn of debt, and its 2031 bonds yield about 16% ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/explainerwhy-coreweaves-troubles-are-rattling-the-ai-boom-4952500)).

Now look at Nova Scotia. On Friday, Anthropic leased the first 95 MW phase of an EverWind campus at Point Tupper for at least 20 years, due late 2027 ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/nova-scotia/nova-scotia-to-host-massive-data-centre-to-power-anthropic-s-ai-assistant-claude-9.7383129)). It is Anthropic's first in Canada ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-signs-lease-for-first-canadian-data-center-4954967)). EverWind plans four wind farms, though the provincial grid will be the main supply until they're built ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/nova-scotia/nova-scotia-to-host-massive-data-centre-to-power-anthropic-s-ai-assistant-claude-9.7383129)). The partners have pledged to pay all connection and upgrade costs so existing customers don't see rate hikes ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-anthropic-signs-onto-10-billion-nova-scotia-data-centre-project/)).

One site waited on the grid. The other showed up with a plan and the checkbook to pay for it.

## The investor angle

**Who benefits.** Whoever controls power that's ready to use, or can pay to make it ready. Hyperscalers with big cash flows (Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta, Amazon) are far less exposed to the credit squeeze ([investingLive, via FT](https://investinglive.com/news/only-a-third-of-the-us-data-centre-pipeline-looks-credible-as-ai-financing-strains-build/)).

**Who carries the risk.** Builders that need outside capital: CoreWeave (CRWV), Nebius (NBIS), IREN, Cipher (CIFR), TeraWulf (WULF) ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/explainerwhy-coreweaves-troubles-are-rattling-the-ai-boom-4952500); [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/only-a-third-of-the-us-data-centre-pipeline-looks-credible-as-ai-financing-strains-build/); tickers: [Portfolio Terminal](https://portfolio-terminal.com/blog/neocloud-stocks-what-is-a-neocloud)). Second-order: Nvidia (NVDA), SK Hynix, Samsung and TSMC if orders slip ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/only-a-third-of-the-us-data-centre-pipeline-looks-credible-as-ai-financing-strains-build/)).

**Private.** Nscale is aiming for a New York IPO at about $35bn, which bankers call hard near-term ([HT/FT](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/americas-10-trillion-ai-data-centre-boom-faces-delays-rising-costs-and-investor-concerns-101792343327657.html)). Also EverWind and Anthropic.

**Timeline.** Loughton: the early-to-mid 2030s ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/18/microsoft-drops-huge-ai-datacentre-project-in-essex-over-power-supply-concerns)). Point Tupper: late 2027, then 325 MW more in 2028, subject to conditions ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-anthropic-signs-onto-10-billion-nova-scotia-data-centre-project/)). Dates slip. Patient money plans for that.

> **SI 101 — Neocloud.** A newer cloud company that rents out Nvidia computing power to AI developers. It spends heavily up front on chips and buildings, often with debt ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/explainerwhy-coreweaves-troubles-are-rattling-the-ai-boom-4952500)). A landlord for intelligence, with a mortgage.

## What this looks like by 2036

*Our judgment, not fact.* The next decade of AI may be decided as much by utilities and project finance as by model labs. By 2036, the scarcest asset in AI could be a signed grid connection with clean power behind it. Grids can speed up and efficiency can cut demand, so hold this view loosely. Still, this week the market got a good, clear look at what scarce means. It's worth remembering.

_Not investment advice. Just a clearer view._
