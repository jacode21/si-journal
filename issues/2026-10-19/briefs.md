# Briefs

### Anthropic sets an IPO clock
Tags: AI

Anthropic plans to file publicly with the SEC on Oct 26, start its roadshow Nov 9 and price the week after. It's aiming to raise up to $100bn at a $2trn valuation. All of that comes from one person familiar with the process, so treat the dates as a plan ([IFR](https://www.ifre.com/equities/2499407/anthropic-pushes-ahead-with-mid-november-ipo)). Third-quarter revenue was $16.8bn, up 45% on the quarter, after more than 140% the quarter before. New York City's comptroller has asked the company to pause (same).

### China's rare-earth truce has an end date
Tags: Policy

The rare-earth export controls China announced last October are suspended until Nov 10, 2026 ([CIRS](https://www.cirs-group.com/en/chemicals/china-temporarily-suspends-export-controls-on-key-raw-materials-including-rare-earths-lithium-batteries-and-diamond)). That's three weeks from today. On Oct 15, China's commerce ministry said it would keep approving rare-earth export licenses for the EU, and asked Brussels to return the favor on dual-use cases ([China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202610/15/WS6ad089c1e4b06d4aa0562fd5.html)). The suspended package includes controls on magnet-making technology, which motors and robots need. What happens on that date matters well beyond China.

### Quantum computing's costliest step gets cheaper
Tags: Quantum

Useful quantum computers need "magic states," and making them has been enormously expensive. Some estimates put it at up to 95% of a machine's capacity. Google says it has now shown a cheaper method, "cultivation," working on its Willow chip, with errors about 40× lower ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/quantum-computing/magic-state-cultivation-quantum-computing/); [Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aee7961)). The catch: only about 8% of attempts pass every check ([Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-magic-state-cleanup-errors.html)). It's a proof of principle, not a product.

### Starship asks to fly a lot more
Tags: Space

SpaceX is currently allowed 25 Starship launches a year from Starbase, Texas. The FAA has opened an environmental review of a plan for up to 100 a year, and eventually up to 456 a year on weekdays only, "within several years" ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20963/notice-of-intent-to-prepare-a-programmatic-environmental-impact-statement-peis-open-public-scoping)). Public meetings are Nov 17–19. If rockets become routine, getting things into orbit could become something that's just there, like the grid.
